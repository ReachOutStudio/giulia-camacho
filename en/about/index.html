About

I'm a Brazilian editorial and lifestyle photographer. I built my career in London photographing people on the street and at home: editorials, couples and families, many of them Brazilians far from home.

In 2026 I'm moving back to São Paulo with the same eye: natural light, the city as a set, and people at ease in front of the camera. I work with brands, magazines and agencies, and I still photograph portraits and families.
